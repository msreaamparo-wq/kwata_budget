KWARTA - NATIVE ANDROID APP

IMPORTANT: This is a Flutter Android source project, NOT the website version.

TO BUILD YOUR APK ON WINDOWS:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Ensure Flutter + Android Studio/Android SDK are installed.
3. Open the extracted folder.
4. Double-click BUILD-APK.bat.
5. Wait for BUILD SUCCESSFUL.
6. Find the APK in build\app\outputs\flutter-apk\app-release.apk.
7. Copy the APK to your phone and install it.

The app saves records to SQLite on the phone first. Supabase is used for cloud sync.
The first sign-in requires internet. After signing in, records can be added offline.

See README.md for full setup notes.
